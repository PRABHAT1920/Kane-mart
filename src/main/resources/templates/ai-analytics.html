How KaneMart Analytics Works

Understand how the dashboard supports inventory decisions.

D

Demand Estimation

Uses recorded order quantities to calculate a baseline demand estimate for the selected period.

S

Sales Analysis

Groups recorded product sales by date and calculates sales summary metrics and a basic trend.

I

Inventory Support

Helps identify products that may require stock review based on current quantity and estimated demand.
